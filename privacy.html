Privacy Policy

Content Factory is a private tool used only by its operator to upload and schedule videos on YouTube channels the operator owns.

What it accesses

With the operator's consent it uses the YouTube upload and management permissions (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl) to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the operator's own channels.

What it stores

An OAuth refresh token, kept only in a local file on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google and YouTube.

Sharing

No data is collected from other people, sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
